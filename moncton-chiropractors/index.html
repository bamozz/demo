What we treat

Targeted chiropractic care for the conditions that slow you down.

Whether you're recovering from a sports injury, managing chronic pain, or dealing with the aftermath of an accident, we provide hands-on treatment tailored to your situation.

C

Chiropractic Care

Spinal assessment and manual adjustment for neck pain, back pain, and headaches. Our approach is evidence-informed and adapted to each patient's presentation and comfort level.

S

Sports Injury Treatment

From acute strains to recurring overuse injuries, we work with active patients to restore function and reduce the risk of re-injury — so you can get back to training or competition sooner.

A

Accident Recovery

Whiplash, soft-tissue injury, and post-collision discomfort treated with structured, documented care. We work alongside your insurance or WHSCC claim where applicable.

P

Postural Assessment

Desk workers, drivers, and anyone dealing with chronic stiffness can benefit from a full postural screen. We identify the root cause, not just the site of pain.

R

Rehabilitation & Exercises

We give you specific corrective exercises to do at home between visits — building strength and stability so the improvements last well beyond the treatment room.

N

New Patient Assessment

Your first visit includes a thorough health history, physical exam, and a clear explanation of what we found and how we propose to treat it — before anything begins.

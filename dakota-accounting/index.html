What I do

Clear, reliable accounting services for small businesses and individuals in South Dakota.

I focus on the three areas where accurate numbers matter most — and where getting them wrong costs you time, money, and sleep.

B

Bookkeeping

Monthly and quarterly bookkeeping that keeps your records clean and your cash flow picture clear. I reconcile accounts, categorise transactions, and deliver reports you can actually read and act on.

P

Payroll Services

I handle payroll processing for small businesses — calculating deductions, preparing payroll runs, and managing the filings that come with them, so you pay your people right and stay compliant.

T

Tax Preparation

Business and personal tax returns prepared accurately and filed on time. I work year-round on tax planning as well — not just at filing season — so we're ahead of problems rather than reacting to them.

A

Account Cleanup

Took over a business with messy books, or let things slide for a few months? I've helped plenty of clients get their records back in order — without judgment, just methodical cleanup and a clear path forward.

C

CFO Advisory

For growing businesses that need more than transaction processing, I offer regular financial reviews and planning conversations — a sounding board with the numbers to back it up.

N

New Client Onboarding
